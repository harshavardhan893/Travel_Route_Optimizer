Understanding the Problem

The Travelling Salesperson Problem (TSP) is an optimization problem where a salesperson must visit every city exactly once and return to the starting city while minimizing the total travel distance.

What is the problem?

Finding the shortest possible round trip connecting multiple cities.

Why does it matter?

It is useful in delivery services, logistics, transportation and route planning.

What will we compute?

We calculate routes and total distances using four different algorithms.
